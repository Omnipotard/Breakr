The firmware runs here as it is, compiled to WebAssembly. Turn your sound on.
Drop a WAV, MP3 or AIFF loop here: it becomes one of your breaks (up to 8), sliced on its hits. For full control, use the break editor.

Start here

  • PLAY (or Space) plays the pattern: the break, in order.
  • White keys play the 16 slices, black keys hold the 11 punch effects.
  • Hold HOME: the help of the page you are on.
  • Turn K4 on PLAY: the dice take over.

Computer keyboard

  • Slices / steps: A…K (1-8), Z…, (9-16)
  • Punch effects: 1…0, -
  • Space play · Enter rec · Tab home · ←→ select · ↑↓ tempo
  • Knobs: drag up / down, or the mouse wheel.

The pages

HOME play · SEQ steps · SEL slicing · EDIT chop a slice · ENV amp · LFO dice & generator · ARP flex patterns · FX master effects · GLO tempo & system · SAVE projects & chains. Press a page's button again for its next page.
